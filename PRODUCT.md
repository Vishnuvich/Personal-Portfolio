## Users

Primary: founders and owners of small and mid-sized businesses who need a website, Shopify store, ad funnel or custom tool built by someone who ships real software. They are comparing a freelancer, an agency and an in-house hire, and want one accountable person who is both technical and commercial.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Vishnu Surendran, who is simultaneously a senior front-end engineer, an entrepreneur building his own products, and the owner of a digital marketing agency. The site exists to turn a visiting business owner into a conversation (email, call, LinkedIn message). Success is a qualified enquiry.

An agency owner who personally writes the production code. Most agencies sell marketing and outsource the build, most developers cannot own the funnel. Vishnu can ship the site, the store, the tracking and the ads on one accountable thread.
